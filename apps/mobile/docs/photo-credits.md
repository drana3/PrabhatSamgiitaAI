## Ananda Marga (22)

Ananda Marga's own photographs from anandamarga.org (the gallery, and two news stories), chosen by Dada, who is authorised to use them. Licence note for every entry: "Ananda Marga's own photograph, used with the organisation's permission". The credit line below is shown on About / Credits. Not included on purpose: gallery photo 108 (removed from the site gallery at Dada's request) and all other candidates.

Pictures are never upscaled (cover: long side at most 1400 px; thumb: at most 480 px wide). `lowRes` = long side under 1000 px (140: 636x848, 164: 800x600): only for small covers and thumbnails, never a full-bleed hero.

People tag: `none` nobody, `far` small or from behind or mid distance, `close` identifiable adults up close, `children` any child or youth (150 shows a young singer on stage, tagged `children` to be safe). `close` and `children` photos are never picked for generic covers: only when the caller opts in with `photoForSeed(seed, mood, { allowPeople: true })` (planned for Stories / About). Calm moods (night, long, morning) prefer `none` / `far` even then. `lowRes` photos are picked only with `{ size: "small" }`.
