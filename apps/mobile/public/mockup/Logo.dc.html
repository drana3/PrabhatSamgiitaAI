Recommendation

01 Sunrise Staff

  • It says both words. The sun is Prabhat, the staff lines are Samgiita, and nothing is borrowed from the Ananda Marga sun and hands.
  • It is the sturdiest small. A half disc and three bars stay readable at 16 px and in any single colour.
  • It sits in the family. Orange sun over forest bars on light, gold over sand on dark, so it belongs with the app without echoing the movement mark.
