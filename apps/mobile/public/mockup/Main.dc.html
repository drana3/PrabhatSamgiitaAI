Harmonium chords

Three keysat once

1Two hands, two jobs

Your right hand plays the keys. Your left hand pumps the bellows with a steady push and pull. If your harmonium has a drone stop, open it: Sa then hums under the whole song.

2A chord is three keys

Press a root key, the key 4 steps above it and the key 7 steps above it, all together. Count every key, black ones too. In a minor chord the middle key is 3 steps above the root, not 4.

3Letters tell you when

The letter above a word is the chord to press on that word. Hold it until the next letter appears.
